System concept

How BannedBand is intended to work

A consent-led support pathway connecting a wearable, agreed venue responses and recovery tools—subject to technical validation, service design and co-governance.

Important: This is a proposed system architecture, not a statement that all components are operational.

Safeguards at each step

1. Enrolment

Explain choices in the person’s preferred language, avoid unnecessary identity data, and record a clear purpose and consent pathway.

2. Detection

Prefer the least revealing signal capable of delivering the agreed support. Do not expose a person’s status to bystanders.

3. Response

Use a pre-agreed, mana-enhancing response. Access should be role-based, logged and limited to trained people.

4. Ongoing support

Give participants and approved whānau/support people usable access, correction, withdrawal and deletion routes.

Governance before technology

For Māori data, individual consent alone is not enough. A pilot should agree who holds decision rights, where data resides, how collective interests are represented, what uses are prohibited, and how the arrangement can be stopped.
