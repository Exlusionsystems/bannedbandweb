Help shape BannedBand responsibly

We are interested in partnerships spanning lived experience, Māori health and governance, Pacific and Asian communities, harm minimisation, clinical services, venue operations, privacy, security and evaluation.

Partnership principles

  • Engage early, before decisions are locked in
  • Recognise expertise, time and intellectual contribution
  • Define decision rights and benefit sharing
  • Do not imply endorsement without explicit approval
  • Make it possible to challenge, pause or stop unsafe work

Priority contributors

  • People with lived and whānau experience
  • Iwi, hapū and Māori health/data-governance partners
  • Chinese, Pacific and other community organisations
  • Gambling-harm and clinical providers
  • Privacy, cybersecurity and accessibility specialists
